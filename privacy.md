# Anon-it Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Anon-it does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Your documents

PDFs you open in Anon-it are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, and the app makes no network connections. The document is held in memory while you work on it. The redacted copy is saved to the app's temporary storage only so you can share it, and is deleted when you start over or reopen the app.

## Settings

The app remembers your chosen settings (which details to redact and the sensitivity) on your device.

## Analytics and tracking

Anon-it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
