# Anon-it: PDF Redaction

Anon-it finds personal details in a PDF and permanently blacks them out, entirely on your iPhone.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my document uploaded anywhere?**
No. Everything is processed on your device and the app makes no network connections.

**Can the hidden text be recovered?**
No. Every page with a redaction is rebuilt as an image with the hidden details removed, so the text can't be copied, searched or recovered. Pages with nothing to hide keep their selectable text.

**Something wasn't detected, or was hidden by mistake.**
Automatic detection is very good but not perfect. Use "Redact more…" to hide any word or phrase everywhere, swipe an item to un-redact it, or change the sensitivity. Always review the redacted PDF before you share it.

**Where is my redacted PDF saved?**
Only where you choose: save it to Files or share it from the share sheet. The app's temporary copy is deleted when you start over.
